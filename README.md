## Research & team work

**📄 Predictive Driver Positioning System** · *Published, IEEE ETCC 2026*
> *Optimizing for the Driver: A Predictive Positioning System to Enhance Welfare in Gig Networks*

Tells gig drivers where to wait across **263 NYC zones**. XGBoost forecasts 15-minute demand (**R² 0.9405**, NRMSE 1.96%), then a tuned multi-criteria model scores each zone on revenue, idle time, saturation and fuel cost. Result: **21–78% higher per-driver earnings** than the baseline strategies. &nbsp;`Python` `XGBoost` `Pandas`

**🌱 SproutBox** · *Team project*
A two-sided marketplace connecting home microgreen growers with restaurants and wellness brands. It has role-based dashboards, order tracking, earnings management, and a full schema for the grow → collect → sell workflow. &nbsp;`React` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL`
